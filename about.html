About

From Pavement to Premium

Hollowpoint merges underground creativity with elevated quality for a new generation of urban style.

Our Story

We started by printing graphics in a one-room studio after skate sessions. What began as small runs for local crews evolved into a full streetwear line worn globally.


Every garment is tested for movement, durability, and silhouette so it looks sharp from daytime grinds to midnight sets.

What We Stand For

Authenticity: No trend-chasing, only identity-first design.

Function: Utility details that work in the real city.

Scarcity: Limited drops to keep each release meaningful.

Community: Collaborations with skaters, dancers, and visual artists.

Materials + Fit

We use heavyweight cotton blends, durable ripstop fabrics, and weather-ready outer shells. Fits range from oversized to relaxed and are designed for layering.

Beyond Product

Hollowpoint supports local skate spaces and music collectives through recurring capsule collabs and event pop-ups across major cities.
